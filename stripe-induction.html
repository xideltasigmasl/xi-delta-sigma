Our story · Our sisterhood

Who we are

Xi Delta Sigma (ΞΔΣ) is a Second Life sorority rooted in sisterhood, empowerment, leadership, and service. We build genuine connections, support one another, and serve our community while carrying ourselves with confidence, class, and integrity.

Our history

Founded on November 20, 2024, Xi Delta Sigma began with Founder Big Sister Monarch and Co-Founder Big Sister Luxe. Our sisterhood brings women together through shared purpose, loyalty, and a commitment to uplifting one another.

Our founding line, The Imperials, followed on March 22, 2025—a milestone in the growing story of Xi Delta Sigma. Each new Stripe takes her first step into that story by committing to represent, support, and strengthen the organization.
